# EmptyState

What a list or page shows before it has data, or when a filter matches nothing.

- Consumer provides `title` (what's missing), `description` (one sentence on why it matters or what to do), an `icon` and `actions` (the primary Button that fixes it).
- First-use empty: invite action ("Create your first invoice"). No-results empty: offer to clear filters, no primary button.
- No illustrations or jokes; one icon from the Icon set.
